Red Jaboticaba in Blue Ceramic

$6,000

10% of proceeds from each purchase will be donated to local Florida wildlife conservation efforts in your name with permission, or anonymously if preferred.

Red Jaboticaba, also known as Red Hybrid Jaboticaba, is commonly associated with Plinia aureana and related hybrids. It is prized for fruiting sooner than many jaboticabas, often within 3-5 years, and for its reddish-maroon berries. The fruit has the character of a complex grape: mildly sweet, gently spicy, and layered with intriguing notes. White flowers appear from the bark and smell exquisite in springtime, or whenever the right conditions are met. A beautiful piece for collectors who want to add a conversation starter in their lanai, on the balcony, at the entrance of an estate, or amongst other living art pieces. Jaboticaba trees are bonsai-friendly and can be shaped over time with the proper tools, pruning, wiring, and technique.

Founder

Troy Chiappone

Founder and Horticultural Designer

Originally from upstate New York, Troy has planted and cared for more than 100 tropical trees since moving to Southwest Florida five years ago. His work is rooted in sustainable agriculture, composting food waste, and preserving the native plant and animal species of Southwest Florida. He is especially drawn to exotic fruiting trees and vines, a domain he has studied through books and hands-on experience. Above all, he loves being outside in nature, getting his hands dirty, and eating and sharing the fruits of his labor.
