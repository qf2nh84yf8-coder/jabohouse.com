Red Jaboticaba in Blue Ceramic

$5,000

Red Jaboticaba, also known as Red Hybrid Jaboticaba, is commonly associated with Plinia aureana and related hybrids. It is prized for fruiting sooner than many jaboticabas, often within 3-5 years, and for its reddish-maroon berries. The fruit has the character of a complex grape: mildly sweet, gently spicy, and layered with intriguing notes. White flowers appear from the bark and smell exquisite in springtime, or whenever the right conditions are met. A beautiful piece for collectors who want to add a conversation starter in their lanai, on the balcony, at the entrance of an estate, or amongst other living art pieces.
